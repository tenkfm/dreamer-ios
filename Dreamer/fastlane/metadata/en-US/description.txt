Dreamer is a dream journal that helps you understand your dreams.

Dreams fade within minutes of waking. Open Dreamer, tap the orb and tell your dream in your own words. Dreamer writes it down as you speak, even offline, so nothing is lost.

UNDERSTAND WHAT YOUR DREAM MEANS
Dreamer reads your dream and offers an interpretation through three lenses:
- Psychology: what the dream may say about your feelings, worries and hopes
- Spiritual: the symbolic and reflective side of the dream
- Dream book: the classic meanings of the symbols you saw
Choose the lens that speaks to you; the others are one tap away. Listen to your interpretation read aloud, and keep your own thoughts next to it.

SEE YOUR PATTERNS
Your journal keeps every dream with its date, feelings and key symbols. See how many dreams you caught this week, which symbols keep coming back and how your dreams felt over the month. Search your journal by any word.

EXPLORE DREAM SYMBOLS
Look up what falling, teeth, water, being chased and many other symbols can mean, with a reflection question for each.

A PICTURE OF EVERY DREAM
Dreamer paints an image of your dream that you can save or share as a card.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your dreams are yours. Their texts never go into analytics or advertising, you can delete any dream or your whole account at any time, and the journal stays readable offline.

A GENTLE MORNING HABIT
An optional morning reminder helps you catch your dreams before they fade.

Dreamer offers reflection, not medical or psychological advice. If a dream leaves you distressed, please talk to someone you trust or a professional.
