Mooncatch is a dream journal: the user tells a dream, and the app writes it down and offers a reflective interpretation.

SIGNING IN
No demo account is needed. On the "Save your dreams" step of onboarding, choose Sign in with Apple (or enter an email address to receive a sign-in link). A new account is created on first sign-in.

SUBSCRIPTION
After the quiz and sign-in the paywall shows two auto-renewable subscriptions in one group: Yearly with a 7-day free trial, and Weekly. Purchases in review use the sandbox, so no charge is made. "Restore" is on the paywall and "Restore purchases" in Settings. Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links are on the paywall and in Settings.

SEEING AN INTERPRETATION WITHOUT TELLING A REAL DREAM
1. On Home, tap the microphone orb.
2. Tap the keyboard button, paste this sample and close the sheet:
   I was back in my grandmother's house, but the hallway kept getting longer. Every door opened onto another staircase going down, and someone was calling my name from below.
3. Tap "I'm done", then "Skip" (or pick feelings and tap "Interpret my dream").
4. After the four steps, tap "See my interpretation".

AI-GENERATED CONTENT
Interpretations, dream images and the read-aloud voice are generated by AI models on our servers. The app presents them as reflection, never as facts or advice: every interpretation and symbol page ends with "Mooncatch offers reflection, not medical advice." Dreams flagged by our safety check (self-harm, abuse) show a support screen instead of an interpretation, with no image or voice.

PERMISSIONS
- Microphone and speech recognition: only while the Record screen is listening, recognition runs on the device; the user can always type instead.
- App Tracking Transparency: asked once, after an explanation, on the first Home screen. Declining changes nothing in the app.
- Photos (add only): when the user saves a dream image.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Delete account deletes the account and all dreams on the server and on the device.
